CampusEats - How it came to be

The newfound legacy of CampusEats began in early 2021 when the university of Malta suggested to have a main restaurant for University students and staff during break, with the Vassallo group. Despite the initative being considered risky, the University went through with it for the students.

During Campsu Hub's last stage of development, CampusEats underwent production upon opening, the establisment. Students flocked to CampusEats despite the initally small menu

CampusEats - A Success Story

Following CampusEats continued success in 2023, the menu was expanded to include a wider variety of dishes to please student's expectations. The university would then start a program that allows student associations to work with CampusEats as members of staff to gain work experience.
